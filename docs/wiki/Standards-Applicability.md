# Standards Applicability and Tailoring

**MIL-STD-882E:** integrated system safety method. **ISO 12100:** tailored machinery hazard analysis. **ISO 13849-1/-2 and IEC 61508:** conditional safety-control methods. **MIL-STD-1472:** tailored human factors. **SAE ARP4754B and ARP4761A:** voluntarily adopted for SUS-A1 aircraft development and safety assessment. **DO-178C/DO-254:** conditional, not presumed applicable. Environmental, cybersecurity and quality practices require their own tailoring.

## Applicability decision workflow
1. State product boundary, mission, authority and contractual assumptions.
2. Verify edition and obtain legitimate standards access.
3. Identify applicability trigger, exclusions, affected subsystems and required work products.
4. Record tailoring rationale, reviewer and decision status.
5. Link every claim to clause-level mapping and actual evidence.
6. Reassess after mission, architecture, contract or authority changes.

See the [register](https://github.com/jherrodthomas/Project-Susanoo/blob/main/framework/standards-applicability.md). Do not reproduce copyrighted standards clauses or equate MIL-STD-882E risk categories with ARP4761A failure-condition classifications.
