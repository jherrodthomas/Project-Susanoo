## Applicability decision workflow
1. State product boundary, mission, authority and contractual assumptions.
2. Verify edition and obtain legitimate standards access.
3. Identify applicability trigger, exclusions, affected subsystems and required work products.
4. Record tailoring rationale, reviewer and decision status.
5. Link every claim to clause-level mapping and actual evidence.
6. Reassess after mission, architecture, contract or authority changes.

Do not reproduce copyrighted standards clauses or equate MIL-STD-882E risk categories with ARP4761A failure-condition classifications.
