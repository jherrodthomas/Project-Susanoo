# Task 206 — Operating and Support Hazard Analysis (O&SHA)

**Series:** Analysis · **Status:** Draft procedure, tailored for SUS-I1 · **Applicability:** see generated [task applicability and tailoring](MIL-STD-882E-Applicability)

## Purpose
Apply operating and support hazard analysis (o&sha) to the SUS-M1 piloted walker, SUS-A1 observation UAV and integrated docking/ground-support interfaces as relevant.

## Trigger and entry
G3/G5 and procedure changes. Confirm system/configuration baseline, task applicability, roles and available inputs before starting.

## Inputs
Operations and maintenance procedures, CONOPS.

## Execution steps
1. Confirm task objective, affected lifecycle phase and system boundary.
2. Analyze ingress, egress, startup, shutdown, rescue, charging, servicing, lifting, transport, maintenance and foreseeable misuse.
3. Record assumptions, unresolved issues, decisions and responsible owners.
4. Link outcomes to the hazard register, requirements, architecture, verification and change records as applicable.

## Outputs
Operations/support hazards and procedural/design controls.

## Accountable roles
Operations, maintenance and safety leads. Assign named reviewers and approval authority in project records.

## Review and acceptance
Reviewer confirms correct configuration, traceable inputs, reproducible method, documented assumptions, identified findings, and links to verification or risk disposition. Only authorized humans accept residual risk. A completed worksheet is not a certification or risk acceptance.

## PDaC record fields
`task_id`, `artifact_id`, `revision`, `status`, `configuration_id`, `source_ids`, `hazard_ids`, `requirement_ids`, `evidence_ids`, `owner`, `reviewer`, `decision`, `change_request`.

## Change triggers
Reopen on relevant CONOPS, configuration, interface, software, parameter, operation, evidence or incident changes. Record affected links and review disposition.
