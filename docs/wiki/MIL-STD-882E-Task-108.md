# Task 108 — Hazardous Materials Management Plan

**Series:** Management · **Status:** Draft procedure, tailored for SUS-I1 · **Applicability:** see generated [task applicability and tailoring](MIL-STD-882E-Applicability)

## Purpose
Apply hazardous materials management plan to the SUS-M1 piloted walker, SUS-A1 observation UAV and integrated docking/ground-support interfaces as relevant.

## Trigger and entry
Material selection and service changes. Confirm system/configuration baseline, task applicability, roles and available inputs before starting.

## Inputs
Material inventory, SDS, batteries, fuels, hydraulic fluids.

## Execution steps
1. Confirm task objective, affected lifecycle phase and system boundary.
2. Identify substances and exposure pathways; assess storage, leaks, fire, maintenance and disposal; coordinate environmental/health controls.
3. Record assumptions, unresolved issues, decisions and responsible owners.
4. Link outcomes to the hazard register, requirements, architecture, verification and change records as applicable.

## Outputs
Hazardous material inventory, handling and disposal controls.

## Accountable roles
Mechanical, EHS and safety leads. Assign named reviewers and approval authority in project records.

## Review and acceptance
Reviewer confirms correct configuration, traceable inputs, reproducible method, documented assumptions, identified findings, and links to verification or risk disposition. Only authorized humans accept residual risk. A completed worksheet is not a certification or risk acceptance.

## PDaC record fields
`task_id`, `artifact_id`, `revision`, `status`, `configuration_id`, `source_ids`, `hazard_ids`, `requirement_ids`, `evidence_ids`, `owner`, `reviewer`, `decision`, `change_request`.

## Change triggers
Reopen on relevant CONOPS, configuration, interface, software, parameter, operation, evidence or incident changes. Record affected links and review disposition.
