# Program & CONOPS

**Purpose:** Define the mission, operational environment, stakeholders, system boundaries, scenarios, modes and constraints before deriving requirements.

**Authoritative artifact:** [concept/CONOPS.md](https://github.com/jherrodthomas/Project-Susanoo/blob/main/concept/CONOPS.md)

## Procedure
1. Identify intended users, operational objectives, exclusions and operating environment.
2. Draw system context and interface boundaries: pilot, walker, UAV, ground support and observers.
3. Write nominal, degraded, emergency and maintenance scenarios, including transitions.
4. Record assumptions and constraints; separate verified facts from hypotheses.
5. Identify human-system interactions, foreseeable misuse, emergency egress and energy isolation.
6. Review with systems, safety, human factors and UAV leads.
7. Baseline the CONOPS and create traceable stakeholder needs, hazards and validation scenarios.

**Entry:** project charter and initial product concept. **Exit:** reviewed CONOPS, open decisions, scenario IDs and change record. **Change response:** revisit impacted requirements, hazard analyses, interface definitions and test plans.
