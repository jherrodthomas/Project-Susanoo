# Systems Engineering Procedure

**Trigger:** new stakeholder need, baseline change or design discrepancy.

**Inputs:** CONOPS, constraints, hazards, stakeholder needs, configuration.

## Activities
1. Assign stable IDs and provenance to needs and requirements.
2. Write singular, verifiable requirements with conditions, measurable acceptance criteria and rationale.
3. Allocate to SUS-M1, SUS-A1, docking or ground support; capture interfaces in ICDs.
4. Model functions, modes, states and architecture; preserve assumptions and alternatives.
5. Link each requirement to source, architecture element and verification method.
6. Conduct peer review for feasibility, safety consistency and testability.
7. Baseline through a controlled change; flag affected evidence on modification.

**Outputs:** requirements, interface definitions, architecture model, verification cross-reference and review record.

**Exit:** no orphaned released requirements, review disposition recorded, unresolved issues visible. **Owner:** systems lead; safety and UAV leads review relevant allocations.
