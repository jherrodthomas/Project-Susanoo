# SUS-A1 UAV Development and Safety Assessment

**Method:** voluntarily apply relevant SAE ARP4754B aircraft development and ARP4761A safety assessment methods to the unarmed observation UAV. No airworthiness or regulatory approval is implied.

1. Define aircraft functions, operating envelope, interfaces, failure assumptions and operational limitations.
2. Conduct Functional Hazard Assessment (FHA), including UAV–mech interactions.
3. Allocate safety objectives to architecture and subsystems; conduct Preliminary System Safety Assessment (PSSA).
4. Apply FMEA, fault-tree and common-cause analysis where useful, with explicit assumptions.
5. Verify design, implementation, communications, docking and lost-link behaviors.
6. Conduct System Safety Assessment (SSA) against the configured UAV baseline.
7. Review discrepancies, residual aircraft risks, evidence and applicable flight-test permissions.

**Outputs:** aircraft requirements, FHA/PSSA/SSA, FMEA/FTA/CCA, verification evidence and review decisions. **Important:** aircraft failure-condition classifications are not MIL-STD-882E risk categories. DO-178C/DO-254 objectives require separate applicability and assurance decisions.
