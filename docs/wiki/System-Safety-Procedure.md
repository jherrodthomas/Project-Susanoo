# Integrated System Safety Procedure — MIL-STD-882E Method

**Scope:** pilot, walker, hydraulic/electric energy, stability, manipulation, UAV docking, maintenance and foreseeable misuse.

## Activities
1. Establish System Safety Program Plan (SSPP), roles, risk criteria, tracking and decision authorities.
2. Perform Preliminary Hazard List (PHL) from CONOPS scenarios and energy sources.
3. Perform Preliminary Hazard Analysis (PHA) to identify causes, consequences, initial risks and candidate controls.
4. Conduct subsystem/system hazard analyses (SSHA/SHA) as architecture matures.
5. Evaluate operating/support hazards (O&SHA), especially ingress, egress, servicing and maintenance.
6. Prioritize elimination and engineered safeguards; define safety requirements and verification.
7. Maintain hazard records with unique IDs, configuration applicability, mitigations, residual risk and evidence.
8. Record unresolved risk and route acceptance only to authorized humans; never automate acceptance.

**Entry:** CONOPS, operating modes, architecture and assumptions. **Outputs:** SSPP, PHL/PHA/SSHA/SHA/O&SHA, hazard log, safety requirements, evidence and risk disposition. **Exit:** analysis review completed and open risk explicit; completion is not certification.

**Current records:** the [Preliminary Hazard List](../../safety/preliminary-hazard-list.md), generated from [`pdac/items/hazards.yaml`](../../pdac/items/hazards.yaml). Ratings there are proposals until the safety lead reviews them.

Example hazard families: uncontrolled joint movement, fall/overturn, pressure injection, hot surfaces, pilot entrapment, UAV rotor strike, docking release failure, loss of power and misleading flight imagery.
