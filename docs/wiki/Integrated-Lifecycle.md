# Integrated Lifecycle

The project-defined gates are **G0 Concept**, **G1 Requirements**, **G2 Preliminary architecture and hazard assessment**, **G3 Detailed design**, **G4 Simulation/bench readiness**, **G5 Verification and assessment**, **G6 Controlled release**, **G7 Change and sustainment**.

For each gate, record: entry baseline, deliverables, review participants, open issues, decision authority, decision date and next actions. No gate implies physical pilot test authorization.

See [lifecycle source](https://github.com/jherrodthomas/Project-Susanoo/blob/main/framework/lifecycle.md). Maintain distinct approval processes for human-occupied testing and UAV flight operations.
