# Task 302 — Hazard Management Assessment Report (HMAR)

**Series:** Evaluation · **Status:** Draft procedure, tailored for SUS-I1 · **Applicability:** Candidate; confirm against program task selection

## Purpose
Apply hazard management assessment report (hmar) to the SUS-M1 piloted walker, SUS-A1 observation UAV and integrated docking/ground-support interfaces as relevant.

## Trigger and entry
G5/G6. Confirm system/configuration baseline, task applicability, roles and available inputs before starting.

## Inputs
Hazard tracking history, closure evidence and governance.

## Execution steps
1. Confirm task objective, affected lifecycle phase and system boundary.
2. Audit hazard coverage, traceability, status accuracy, review quality, closure evidence and acceptance records
3. log findings.
4. Record assumptions, unresolved issues, decisions and responsible owners.
5. Link outcomes to the hazard register, requirements, architecture, verification and change records as applicable.

## Outputs
Assessment of hazard management completeness and effectiveness.

## Accountable roles
Independent reviewer where feasible. Assign named reviewers and approval authority in project records.

## Review and acceptance
Reviewer confirms correct configuration, traceable inputs, reproducible method, documented assumptions, identified findings, and links to verification or risk disposition. Only authorized humans accept residual risk. A completed worksheet is not a certification or risk acceptance.

## PDaC record fields
`task_id`, `artifact_id`, `revision`, `status`, `configuration_id`, `source_ids`, `hazard_ids`, `requirement_ids`, `evidence_ids`, `owner`, `reviewer`, `decision`, `change_request`.

## Change triggers
Reopen on relevant CONOPS, configuration, interface, software, parameter, operation, evidence or incident changes. Record affected links and review disposition.

**Reference:** MIL-STD-882E with Change 1, Task 302; verify wording, contract applicability and DID requirements against the controlled standard before formal use.
